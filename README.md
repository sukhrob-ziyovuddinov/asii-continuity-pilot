# ASII Continuity Pilot

**From fragmented financial-crime signals to human-reviewed, evidence-supported, institutionally continuous investigations and regulator-ready reporting preparation.**

## Purpose of this repository

This repository presents the **ASII Continuity Pilot** as an external-facing concept and workflow reference for regulated financial-crime teams.

ASII is designed as a **continuity and intelligence overlay, not a rip-and-replace platform**. The core remains jurisdiction-adaptable. Jurisdiction-specific requirements are added as controlled overlays without changing the underlying investigation-continuity architecture.

## Current institutional evaluation track — Tajikistan

Through September 2026, the immediate go-to-market objective is to make the existing ASII Continuity Pilot credible for institutional evaluation in Tajikistan.

This repository does **not** claim that ASII has been approved, adopted, deployed, purchased, or piloted by any Tajikistan regulator or financial institution. The current objective is limited to a controlled technical/compliance evaluation pathway.

See [`TAJIKISTAN_EVALUATION_SCOPE.md`](TAJIKISTAN_EVALUATION_SCOPE.md) for the bounded evaluation model.

## Continuity workflow

1. **Signal intake and normalization**
2. **Risk-context enrichment and evidence linking**
3. **Case workspace creation with linked evidence and rationale**
4. **Analyst investigation and continuity tracking across updates**
5. **Human review, escalation, and accountable disposition**
6. **Reporting preparation for internal governance and regulatory workflows**

The pilot can ingest or reference multiple financial-crime signal types, including transaction-monitoring alerts, KYC/KYB context, sanctions-related signals, Travel Rule handoffs, crypto-risk context, case notes, and supporting evidence.

## Target users

- Financial-crime operations teams at regulated institutions
- AML/CFT and sanctions investigators
- Crypto-risk and transaction-monitoring analysts
- Compliance leadership and MLRO functions
- Risk, governance, and technology owners supporting investigation workflows

## Operating principles

- **Overlay, not rip-and-replace**
- **Human-reviewed operations with accountable ownership**
- **Evidence-supported case development and traceability**
- **Preservation of decision context and investigation history**
- **MLRO-reviewable outputs before formal submission steps**
- **Reporting preparation, not automated regulatory filing**
- **Jurisdiction overlays without fragmentation of the ASII core**
